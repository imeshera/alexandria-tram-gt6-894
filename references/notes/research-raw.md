# Ex-Copenhagen Düwag GT6 (cars 894 and 856): raw research notes

Compiled 2026-10-17. Raw research for the GT6 894 model; the condensed version with the dimension sheet is `heritage-dossier.md`.
Confidence key: **S** = stated by a cited source. **S?** = sourced, but sources conflict or the source is weak (fan database, generic type data). **V** = visual reading of a dated, cited photo. **E** = engineering estimate, to be checked by photogrammetry.
Source tags like [H1] are listed at the end; the numbering is shared with the sibling model repositories, so gaps are expected.

---

## 0. Headline findings

1. **Car 894 is an ex-Copenhagen Düwag GT6 (KS 894 → APTA 894, same number).** It was built by Düwag in Düsseldorf; no Scandia involvement was found. 31 of the 100 Copenhagen cars were delivered as bodyshells and finished in Copenhagen [H3]. Alexandria took delivery in 1972 [H8]. The car was still in service in April 2024, painted **green and white** on city route 4 [H10].
2. **Car 856 (green and white, "old, parked") is also an ex-Copenhagen GT6 (KS 856, 1963).** It was sold in 1971 [H5][H6]. The side numerals that read as "١٠٧" are almost certainly **٨٥٦** (Eastern-Arabic 856). In Naskh script ٨ looks like Λ/1, ٥ like 0 and ٦ like 7, so the number reads as "Λ07"/"107" (V). The same happens on 894 (٨٩٤) and 895 (٨٩٥). The "large curved two-pane windscreen with open door" is most likely the **rear end of the B-section**. A single-ended GT6 has a big curved two-pane rear window, and its last door sits right at the rear corner (V, [H10b]). The car is not derelict. Transphoto lists it as in service, photographed at Karmouz on 18 Mar 2025 [H10b].
3. **Copenhagen's own livery was yellow and cream**, not cream and white. The museum car KS 890 shows an ochre-yellow lower body, a cream window band and upper body, and a grey roof (V, [H12]). Exception: 801 and 802 were delivered **cream with green lining** [H7]. Alexandria simply kept the yellow/ochre and cream ("they kept their Copenhagen paint scheme, which was similar to Alexandria's") [H4]. So the **ochre-orange and cream 894 of 1974 is still essentially the KS livery** (V, [H9a]).
4. **Light blue/white vs yellow on single (non-coupled) cars.** The old single cars are the GT6s.
   - **Yellow (city):** ochre and cream (1970s); yellow, cream and blue stripe (c. 2000s–2010s).
   - **Green and white:** c. 2016 onward, city.
   - **Red and white, and blue and white:** a refurbished GT6 batch for **Raml lines 5/6** (Omar Lotfy street, El-Qa'ed Ibrahim terminus), c. 2017–2019 [H14].
   - The other light-blue single car on the Raml was **601** before 2020.

### 1.3 Front-end and visual features to model (V, from 1974–2025 photos)

- Düwag "PCC-derived" sloped, raked windscreen. A centre pillar divides it into two large panes. Small side cab windows.
- **One round headlight low and centred on the dash.** Small red marker/tail lamps either side, low. Destination box built into the roof dome above the windscreen, with a route-number box beside it. Some cars added a second small box on the roof.
- Rounded roof ends front and rear. Pole base mid-roof on the A-section.
- Fleet number twice on the dash: Western digits on the left (e.g. "894") and Eastern-Arabic on the right (٨٩٤).
- Rear: a large two-pane curved rear window. The last door is at the rear corner of the B-section on the right side.

## Answers to the research questions

- **Builder of 894: Düwag, or licence-built by Scandia?** Düwag. No Scandia link was found in any source. 31 KS cars were delivered as bodyshells and finished in Copenhagen [H3].
- **Which Alexandria cars kept Copenhagen numbers?** All 99 [H3][H17].
- **Converted to single-ended, or given pantographs?** They were always single-ended. No pantograph conversions are documented. A refurbished subset (repainted red and blue/white) moved to the Raml network for lines 5/6, c. 2017–19 [H14]. One door was removed ("the second door") in Alexandria [H14].
- **Copenhagen livery cream/white?** No. KS was **yellow below and cream above** with a grey roof. Only 801 and 802 were delivered cream with green lining [H7][H12].
- **Green in Alexandria: which lines?** None in particular. It was a c. 2016 repaint of city cars, first seen in Mansheya/Bahri [H16].
- **Single cars in light yellow / light blue:**
  - Yellow: city GT6s.
  - Light blue / blue-white: the refurbished GT6s on Raml lines 5/6 (2019) [H14], and heritage car 601 before 2020 [H24].
  - Also seen: Raml Kinki sets in light blue with an orange stripe (2012 background of Commons "Manshiya Tramway (Yellow Tram).JPG").
- **Is 856 "something else", e.g. a museum car?** No. It is a regular ex-KS GT6, seen from its rear end, in green and white, at Karmouz in 2025 [H10b].
